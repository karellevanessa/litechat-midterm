What is Tutor mode?

Tutor mode turns Petal into a study tutor. It helps you understand your coursework, not only get an answer.

When it is on, Petal:

  • names the key idea the question needs, in plain words;
  • explains step by step, one idea at a time, and defines new terms;
  • gives the full solution, step by step, when you ask for it;
  • ends with a short check question or a practice problem.

When it is off

You get short, direct answers. Use this when you only need a quick fact or you already know the method.

Good to know

  • It is on by default. Turn it on or off for each study session with the Tutor mode switch under the chat.
  • Tutor replies are longer, so they cost a little more. A typical tutor reply still costs less than 1 cent.
  • AI can make mistakes. Check the information it generates, and ask your teacher when you are unsure.
